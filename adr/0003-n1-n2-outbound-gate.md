# ADR 0003 — N1/N2 and Explicit Outbound Gate

Status: Accepted

## Decision

V1 global maturity is N1 or N2. AI generation is a distinct internal entity. Only an authenticated operator's send command creates a customer-visible operator message.

`Take over` can reduce an N2 conversation to N1 until close.

## Consequences

Direct AI-send is structurally absent and negatively tested.
